Privacy policy

Laptop Drive Backup is a personal desktop setup. Its operator selects what to back up. Restic encrypts the backup contents and metadata on the laptop before rclone sends them to the operator's selected Google Drive account.

Google Drive access

The setup requests file-specific Google Drive access to create and manage its backup repository. It uses Google account information and storage quota to verify the backup destination. Google data is used for backup, integrity checks and recovery. It is not sold, used for advertising or sent to a separate application server.

Storage and control

Rclone stores Google authorization tokens locally on the laptop. Encrypted snapshots are retained in Google Drive according to the operator's selected retention settings. The operator can remove the app's access in Google Account settings and delete the backup repository from Google Drive. Deleting the backup repository removes the cloud recovery copy.

Website

These pages do not request Google account access, collect backup data or set application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process ordinary web request data under GitHub's privacy terms.
